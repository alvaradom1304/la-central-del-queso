# Continuous Sales-Driven Expansion Engine

## 1. Sales-Driven Growth Flywheel
**Overview:** High-margin retail sales of flagship Queso Semiduro at the Feria fund vacuum-packaging and digital automation, which unlocks delivery capacity to capture recurring B2C and high-volume B2B wholesale nodes.

**Flywheel Loop:**

- Acquire high-margin customers at the Feria del Agricultor through physical tasting and direct trust-building.

- Convert physical buyers to digital recurring customers via QR codes on packaging routing to WhatsApp.

- Reinvest high-margin retail cash flow into vacuum-sealing equipment and automated n8n order-processing workflows.

- Leverage extended product shelf-life and automated admin to pitch and supply local B2B sodas and restaurants.

- Use stable B2B recurring volume to optimize delivery routes, lowering unit delivery costs and funding further geographic expansion.


## 2. Capacity & Reinvestment Triggers
| Milestone | Capacity Upgrade Unlocked |
|---|---|

| **Achieve 20% conversion of Feria customers to digital WhatsApp database within 3 months** | Launch scheduled local delivery route and transition 100% of flagship products to vacuum-sealed packaging. |

| **Secure 5 active B2B sodas or restaurants buying weekly in the first semester** | Establish dedicated cold-chain transport equipment and transition to automated B2B lead qualification. |

| **Maintain a minimum 35% margin on Queso Semiduro with an Average Order Value (AOV) of 8,500 CRC** | Upgrade to industrial-grade vacuum-sealing machinery and expand raw milk sourcing agreements. |


## 3. Expansion Node Playbooks

### Feria-to-WhatsApp Digital Funnel
- **Strategy:** Convert high-trust physical interactions at the Feria into frictionless, recurring digital sales.
- **Tactics:**

  - Place QR codes on all vacuum-sealed packaging linking to a pre-filled WhatsApp order chat.

  - Offer a first-delivery discount incentive during the physical tasting experience.

  - Deploy n8n conversational AI to parse unstructured WhatsApp order messages into structured Airtable records.


### Route-Optimized B2B Wholesale
- **Strategy:** Target local food businesses to secure high-volume, predictable weekly recurring revenue.
- **Tactics:**

  - Deliver curated Tasting Sample Packs of Queso Semiduro directly to local sodas and restaurants.

  - Use Next.js landing page forms to capture and automatically qualify wholesale leads based on delivery zones.

  - Enforce a strict 'no-fiado' policy for the first 3 orders, transitioning to a 7-day limit thereafter.


### Convenience-Seeking Subscription Club
- **Strategy:** Maximize household lifetime value (LTV) through scheduled bi-weekly delivery bundles.
- **Tactics:**

  - Promote high-margin bundles like 'Combo Tradicional' and 'Kit Desayuno Familiar' to increase AOV.

  - Send automated, personalized WhatsApp reorder reminders on scheduled delivery days.

  - Implement a 'Bring a Neighbor' referral discount to increase customer density on active delivery routes.



## 4. Expansion Governance & Risk Controls
| Risk Area | Guardrail / Policy |
|---|---|

| **Uncontrolled 'Fiados' (Accounts Receivable)** | Enforce a strict 7-day credit cap managed by automated n8n WhatsApp reminders, with immediate service suspension upon non-payment. |

| **Loss of Artisanal Warmth** | Keep production, physical Feria relations, and final delivery 100% human; restrict AI strictly to backend administrative tasks. |

| **Inventory Bloat and Overselling** | Maintain real-time inventory synchronization between the central Airtable database and the Next.js digital catalog. |
