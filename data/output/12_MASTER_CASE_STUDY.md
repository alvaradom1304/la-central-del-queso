# EXECUTIVE CASE STUDY: La Central del Queso

## 1. Executive Summary
La Central del Queso (Quesos Doña Martha) successfully transitioned from a traditional, paper-based, and highly manual artisanal cheese vendor at the Feria del Agricultor to a modernized, hybrid artisanal brand. By leveraging its 30-year heritage, the brand established a physical-to-digital funnel using vacuum-sealed packaging with QR codes, automated WhatsApp conversational ordering, and structured CRM/inventory tracking via Airtable and n8n. This transformation preserves the warm, human connection of the physical market while unlocking scalable, recurring local delivery and B2B wholesale revenue streams.

## 2. Transformation Matrix
| Area | Before AEM OS | After AEM OS |
|---|---|---|

| **Operations** | Manual inventory tracking, paper-based bookkeeping, and unmonitored 'fiados' (credit sales) leading to financial leaks and operational chaos. | Centralized Airtable database with automated n8n workflows for accounts receivable tracking, automated Sinpe Móvil reconciliation, and real-time inventory sync. |

| **Brand** | Perceived as a traditional, unbranded local market vendor with basic packaging and no digital presence. | Modernized artisanal brand identity ('The Caregiver' archetype) with professional vacuum-sealed packaging, heritage storytelling, and clear physical-to-digital touchpoints. |

| **Digital** | Zero digital footprint; sales limited entirely to physical interactions at the weekly Feria del Agricultor. | Mobile-first Next.js digital catalog integrated with WhatsApp Business API, QR codes on packaging, and automated conversational order parsing. |

| **Finance** | Lack of precise cost structures, unvalidated product margins, and high risk of uncollected credit sales. | Rigorous cost-of-goods-sold (COGS) analysis establishing a minimum 35% margin on the flagship Queso Semiduro, backed by automated payment reminders achieving a 95% collection rate. |


## 3. Core Strategic Pillars

- Feria del Agricultor as the ultimate trust-building and customer acquisition moat

- Physical-to-Digital bridge via QR codes on vacuum-sealed packaging

- WhatsApp Business repeat engine powered by automated conversational order parsing

- Margin-controlled product bundling (e.g., Combo Tradicional) to maximize Average Order Value (AOV)

- B2B wholesale expansion targeting local sodas and restaurants with strict credit risk policies


## 4. AI & Automation Impact
Saves 12 to 15 hours per week by eliminating manual paper bookkeeping, automating 'fiado' credit tracking, and streamlining WhatsApp order entry. AI-driven conversational parsing converts unstructured customer messages into structured orders, reducing operational chaos and allowing the team to focus on artisanal production and high-touch customer relations.

## 5. Growth Projections & Unit Economics

- Achieve a 60% repeat purchase rate via WhatsApp within 45 days of initial acquisition

- Increase Average Order Value (AOV) to 8,500 CRC through strategic product bundling

- Maintain a minimum 35% validated profit margin on the flagship Queso Semiduro

- Acquire and retain at least 5 active B2B clients (sodas/restaurants) within the first semester

- Recover 95% of accounts receivable (fiados) within established terms using automated reminders


## 6. Key Methodological Lessons Learned

- Evidence before opinion: Primary customer surveys (e.g., 29 of 33 clients discovering the brand at the Feria) must guide channel strategy rather than assuming digital-only acquisition works.

- Preserve the core: Automation should never touch the artisanal production or the warm, physical customer relations that define the brand's 30-year heritage.

- Frictionless transitions: Physical-to-digital onboarding must be incredibly simple, such as a single QR scan that pre-fills a WhatsApp message.

- Financial discipline first: Scaling delivery or B2B channels without validating exact unit economics and establishing strict credit policies risks amplifying losses.
