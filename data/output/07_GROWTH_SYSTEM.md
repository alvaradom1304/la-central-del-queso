# Growth Systems & Playbook

## 1. Physical-to-Digital Growth Funnel
- **1. Awareness:** Feria del Agricultor physical stand, leveraging Doña Martha's 30-year family tradition, warm human interaction, and sensory appeal of live product tasting.
- **2. Acquisition (Tasting):** First-time physical purchase at the Feria stand driven by direct tasting of the flagship Queso Semiduro and immediate trust-building.
- **3. Digital Onboarding:** Scanning a QR code on the vacuum-sealed packaging or stand banner, which routes the customer to a pre-filled WhatsApp chat with a discount incentive for their first home delivery order.
- **4. Retention Loop:** A bi-weekly scheduled delivery program coordinated via automated WhatsApp conversational order parsing, keeping customers stocked without requiring them to travel.
- **5. Referral System:** A 'Bring a Neighbor' word-of-mouth incentive program offering a free Natilla or discount on the next order when a customer refers a new household on the same delivery route.

## 2. Margin-Controlled Bundle Matrix

### Combo Tradicional de la Feria
- **Components:** Queso Semiduro (Flagship), Natilla Artesanal, Pan con Queso
- **Margin & AOV Rationale:** Combines the high-margin flagship Queso Semiduro with high-rotation, low-cost artisanal complements to increase Average Order Value (AOV) while maintaining an overall margin above 35%.

### Kit Desayuno Familiar
- **Components:** Queso Turrialba Artesanal, Queso Semiduro, Natilla Artesanal
- **Margin & AOV Rationale:** Encourages multi-cheese purchasing by pairing the heritage Turrialba with the flagship Semiduro, maximizing delivery route profitability and increasing the average transaction size.


## 3. B2B Acquisition Strategy
- **Outreach Concept:** Pitching local sodas and restaurants on upgrading their dishes with authentic, 30-year traditional Costa Rican cheese that melts better and tastes richer than industrial alternatives, delivered directly to their business at competitive wholesale prices.
- **Tasting Sample Pack:** A curated sample box containing vacuum-sealed portions of Queso Semiduro (for melting/grating tests) and Queso Turrialba, paired with a product spec sheet and a QR code linking to the wholesale pricing catalog.
- **Credit Risk Policy (Fiados):** Strict 'no-fiado' policy for the first 3 B2B orders (must be paid upfront via Sinpe Móvil or cash on delivery). Subsequent credit is capped at a 7-day limit, managed via the automated n8n/Airtable accounts receivable reminder system.

## 4. Unit Economics & Key Growth Metrics
- **Lifetime Value (LTV):** LTV = (Average Order Value * Purchase Frequency per Year * Average Customer Lifespan in Years) * Gross Margin %
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** CAC = (Total Marketing, Tasting Samples, and Feria Stand Operational Costs / Number of New Customers Acquired)
- **Repeat Purchase Rate Goal:** 60% of acquired customers making at least one repeat purchase via WhatsApp within 45 days.
- **Average Order Value (AOV) Goal:** An Average Order Value of 8,500 CRC, driven by strategic product bundling and recurring delivery subscriptions.
